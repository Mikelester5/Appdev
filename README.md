# Superman

**Superman** is a fictional superhero who appears in comic books published by *DC Comics*.  
He was created by writer **Jerry Siegel** and artist **Joe Shuster**, and first appeared in *Action Comics #1* in 1938.

### Fun Facts

- He is one of the most recognizable fictional characters in the world.  
- His symbol **“S”** stands for *hope* on his home planet Krypton.  
- Superman works at the *Daily Planet* newspaper.
